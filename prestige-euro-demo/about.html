PRECISION UNDER THE HOOD

Every detail matters.

From the diagnostic tools we use to the parts we fit, nothing in this workshop is an afterthought — because on a European engine, the details are exactly where things go wrong.

SINCE 2015

Proudly serving the Nelson Tasman region.

We have been proudly offering our specialist services to the Nelson Tasman Region since 2015. Owner James Martyn initially noticed a need for specialist European mechanics in Nelson while living in Christchurch and observing car owners driving south for servicing and repairs. James made the move to sunny Nelson, and a decade later, his loyal and returning clientele is a testament to his and his team's ongoing passion, clear communication and top-class service.

OUR EXPERTISE

Meticulous by nature.

At Prestige Euro Works, we understand the meticulous attention to detail required to maintain and service high-performance and luxury European vehicles. Our team has many years of experience within the European automotive industry.

We are committed to investing in the most current diagnostic, performance tuning and mechanical equipment to ensure we can offer all necessary services for even the most modern European vehicles at a realistic price.

Our technicians hold extensive knowledge from their experience working with a wide range of European automobiles, including Audi, BMW, Land Rover, Mercedes-Benz, Mini, Porsche, Skoda, Volvo, Volkswagen and more. This expertise, combined with our dedication to European automotive engineering, ensures your vehicle receives exceptional care.

Should you have any questions or wish to schedule a booking, please do not hesitate to contact us by phone or email. We look forward to assisting you with your European car needs.
