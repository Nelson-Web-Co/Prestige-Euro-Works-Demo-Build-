Economy Tuning On Diesels

+

Our Economy Remapping service enhances engine efficiency and power, reducing the effort required in various driving conditions. This leads to a decrease in throttle load and allows for earlier and prolonged use of higher gears, resulting in significant improvements in fuel efficiency.

Typically, most diesel vehicles experience an 8–10% improvement, and sometimes even more, depending on individual driving habits. This service is also highly beneficial for vehicle fleets, where the cumulative savings can be substantial.

DPF & EGR Solutions For Most Vehicles

+

At Prestige Euro Works, we have developed an effective solution for problematic EGR and DPF systems that can become clogged with carbon deposits, leading to failures, blockages, error codes, reduced power, and poor performance. Our EGR and DPF disable software solution offers a straightforward and efficient method for enhancing your engine's efficiency.

Our EGR disabling solution, implemented by reprogramming the vehicle's ECU, leads to reduced engine temperatures, improved throttle response, and better fuel economy. This process also contributes to extending engine life by minimising oil contamination and carbon deposits.

For off-road use only.
