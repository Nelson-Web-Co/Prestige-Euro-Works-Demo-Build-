HOW IT WORKS

What remapping actually does.

Performance tuning, or "remapping," involves reading your vehicle's standard calibration map through the OBD port or a direct connection on the bench, then adjusting parameters such as fuel pressure, boost pressure (on turbocharged applications), ignition advance, and throttle pedal control to release the engine's true performance.

We've performed tuning on hundreds of vehicles in the Nelson region with continual success.

WILL I SEE THE BENEFIT?

Every vehicle leaves room on the table.

When developing a new vehicle, manufacturers have to compromise on the state of tune to account for every driving condition and region it'll be sold in. We take that initial mapping and safely alter the ECU software to gain real power and torque — improving drivability and, on diesels, fuel economy too.

Depending on the vehicle and ECU type, the process can usually be carried out the same day, though bench programming may take a little longer.

ECONOMY TUNING ON DIESELS

More efficient, not just more power.

Our economy remapping service enhances engine efficiency, reducing the effort required across various driving conditions. This allows earlier and more prolonged use of higher gears, resulting in significant fuel savings.

Most diesel vehicles see an 8–10% improvement, sometimes more depending on driving habits — and it's especially worthwhile for fleets, where the cumulative savings add up fast.
